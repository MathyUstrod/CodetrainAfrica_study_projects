Understanding Your BMI Results

Your BMI is calculated by dividing your weight in kilograms by your height in meters squared (kg/m²). The table below displays the standard weight status categories defined for adults aged 18 and older:

BMI Range Weight Category Associated Health Considerations
Below 18.5 Underweight May indicate malnutrition, vitamin deficiencies, or osteoporosis.
18.5 - 24.9 Healthy Weight Associated with lower risks of chronic disease and optimal health.
25.0 - 29.9 Overweight Increased risk for type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular issues, and hypertension.
30.0 and Above Obese Higher risk for severe long-term chronic conditions, metabolic syndrome, and heart disease.
